
# 🪙 Coin Collection Game

Welcome to the **Coin Collection Game**, a 3D game developed in Unity using C#. Control a ball, collect coins, unlock bonus coins, and reach the target score to win!

---

## 🎯 About the Game

The main objective is to collect **10 normal coins** while moving around the playing area. Players can also collect a special red coin to unlock 50 blue bonus coins and increase their bonus count.

## ✨ Game Features

- **🟡 Normal Coins:** Collect coins to increase the main score.
- **🔴 Red Coin:** Appears when the score reaches a randomly selected target.
- **🔵 Blue Bonus Coins:** Collecting the red coin spawns 50 blue coins in a circular pattern.
- **📊 Separate Counters:** Normal coins and bonus coins have separate scores.
- **🏆 Win Panel:** Appears when the player collects 10 normal coins.
- **🎉 Final Results:** Displays the final score and the number of bonus coins collected.
- **🧱 Playing Boundary:** Keeps the player within the game area.



## 📋 How to Play

1. Run the game in Unity.
2. Use **W, A, S, D** to move the player.
3. Collect normal coins to increase your score.
4. Find and collect the red coin when it appears.
5. Collect the blue bonus coins to increase your bonus count.
6. Reach **10/10 normal coins** to win the game.
7. View your final score and collected bonus coins on the Win Panel.
